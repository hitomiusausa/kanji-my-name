About Kanji My Name

Kanji My Name is made in Japan by KUGA Inc.

500 names in the dictionary today, with 28,747 possible kanji spellings between them.

Hitomi Kuga — Founder

Native Japanese speaker and Japanese-language teacher. Completed doctoral coursework in linguistics (ABD). Every kanji in this site's dictionary was selected by hand, with a rule that the first choice for any sound must carry a positive meaning — and that no character with an awkward or negative sense is ever used as a default.

Names are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
