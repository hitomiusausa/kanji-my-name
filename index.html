About kanji names

Is this a real Japanese translation of my name?

Foreign names have no single "correct" kanji. Japanese tradition uses ateji (当て字): kanji chosen for their sound, whose meanings you select yourself. Your name becomes both phonetic and poetic — that's the art.

Can I use it for a tattoo?

Many people do! The Premium 4K clean edition gives your artist the exact characters in a high-resolution image to review.

Is the free download really free?

Yes — full-size PNG with a small credit line. The Premium Pack adds 4K, the clean edition, all 13 fonts, and exclusive styles — one small payment, lifetime access.

Is my name sent anywhere?

Never. Everything — the kanji matching, the art, the download — happens entirely in your browser. Your name is not sent to any server, stored, or tracked.
