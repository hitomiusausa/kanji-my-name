Questions about Abraham in kanji

There is no single official kanji for Abraham. This is one sound-based ateji (当て字) option: 恵衣武良波夢, read "e-i-bu-ra-ha-mu", with the meanings "blessing · garment · warrior · goodness · wave · dream". What does 恵衣武良波夢 mean?

Character by character, 恵衣武良波夢 reads 恵 ("blessing", read "e"), 衣 ("garment", read "i"), 武 ("warrior", read "bu"), 良 ("goodness", read "ra"), 波 ("wave", read "ha"), 夢 ("dream", read "mu"). Together they sound like "Abraham" while carrying the meanings blessing · garment · warrior · goodness · wave · dream.
